How hard, warm-up, rest times
  • Weeks 1–2: 2 sets per exercise, stop with about 3 reps left.
  • Week 3 on: 3 sets, stop 1–2 reps short of failure. Curls, raises, triceps and push-ups can go to failure on the last set.
  • Never to failure: split squats, single-leg RDLs, pull-up negatives.
  • Rest 60–90 s on small moves, 2 min on squats, rows and push-ups.
  • Arm circles and band pull-aparts, 20 each
  • Bodyweight squats to a bench, 15
  • Hip hinges, 10
  • One light set of the first exercise
  • Tap a set button when you finish it. Ticks reset each day.

Soya is cheapest in money and calories. Dal, milk and paneer carry many calories with their protein, so count them as food. Soy at these amounts doesn't lower testosterone in men.

Where calories leak

  • Sugar in coffee/tea: 60–90 kcal a cup.
  • Coconut chutney: 2 tbsp ≈ 80–100 kcal. Use sambar, tomato or mint chutney.
  • Ghee roast / oil dosa: +100–150 kcal over plain.
  • Cooking oil: 1 tbsp = 120 kcal. Measure with a spoon.
  • Unlimited rice meals: each refill 200–300 kcal.
  • Parotta 250–300, vada 150–200, biryani 800–1,200.
  • Groundnuts: 30 g = 170 kcal.

Eating out: one plate, protein first, no refills, back on plan at the next meal.

Every Sunday, 5 minutes

Weekly rules

Weigh Mon, Wed, Fri after the toilet, before food. Compare weekly averages, never single weigh-ins.

Weeks 1–2Ignore the scale. The first 2–3 kg are water and stored carbs.
Losing 0.5–1% of body weight a weekChange nothing.
Under 0.5% a week for 2 weeks, waist not shrinkingCheck portions, oil, coffee sugar, weekends first. If those were on plan, remove 150–200 kcal (half a cup of rice) or add 2,000 steps. One change at a time.
Scale flat, waist down, reps upYou're losing fat and gaining muscle. Wait 2 more weeks.
Over 1.2% a week after week 4, or reps dropping for 2 weeksAdd 150–200 kcal of rice or chapati around training.
Every 10–12 weeks1–2 weeks at maintenance (+600 kcal, mostly carbs). Keep training. Expect +1–2 kg of water.
Sleeping under 6.5 h most nightsFix sleep before cutting calories again. Short sleep makes more of the weight you lose come from muscle.
Make it harder

Progression

When every set hits the top of its rep range with 1–2 reps left, move one step right next session and restart at the bottom of the range. Past about 30 reps a set stops working the muscle well, so move up a step.

Push-up
hands on bench → floor → hands on bricks → feet on bench → +5 kg backpack → +10 kg
Vertical pull
5 s negatives → band-assisted → 1 rep + negatives → sets of 3–5 → sets of 8+
Inverted row
bar at chest height → waist → hip → feet on bench → backpack on chest
Split squat
bodyweight → hold 8 kg → backpack + 8 kg → front foot on a plate
RDL / hip thrust
8 kg → backpack → backpack + 8 kg → add pauses
8 kg isolation
12–25 → 25–30 → 2 s pause → add a band

Buy in this order

  1. Resistance band set, about ₹800–1,500
  2. A backpack with books, bottles or sand: free
  3. Digital kitchen scale, about ₹300–500
  4. Optional: adjustable dumbbells, 20 kg+

Deload every 8 weeks: half the sets for one week, timed with the diet break.

Measure

Tracking

  • Weight 3× a week → weekly average
  • Waist at the navel every 2 weeks, relaxed, after a normal breath out
  • Photos every 2 weeks: same spot, light, pose, time
  • Steps daily; sets and reps every session; sleep hours

Health

Checks and supplements

Blood test once now, again at week 26

  • HbA1c and fasting glucose
  • Lipid profile
  • Liver enzymes (ALT/SGPT)
  • TSH
  • Vitamin D and B12

About ₹1,000–2,000 in Chennai. Loud snoring or daytime sleepiness is worth telling a doctor about.

Worth the money

  • Creatine monohydrate 3–5 g daily. Adds 1–2 kg of water at first.
  • Vitamin D only if your test shows it's low.
  • Whey only on days food doesn't reach 140 g.
  • Skip: fat burners, BCAAs.

Drink 3+ litres a day in Chennai heat and don't cut salt to zero.

Because it happened before

Regain plan

Minimum week

  • 2 home sessions, 25 min: push-ups, backpack split squats, band rows, hip thrusts
  • 8,000 steps a day
  • 140 g protein
  • Weigh 3× a week. Never stop weighing.

Restart trigger

Weekly average 2 kg above your lowest → back to weighing food and the full program the next day.

After the goal, hold maintenance for 3+ months.
