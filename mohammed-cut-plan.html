01 · What changed and why

Review of the old plan

Wrong
The timeline math33 kg at 0.6–0.9 kg/week is 37–55 weeks, so 9–13 months. The old plan said 7–9. With two maintenance breaks, plan on about 12 months to 80 kg.
Wrong
200 g proteinThe old menu reached about 130–150 g, so the target was never being met. It also wasn't needed. Muscle retention in a cut is covered by roughly 2 g per kg of goal body weight, which is about 160 g for you. Chasing 200 g costs about ₹40–60 a day and 150+ kcal you could spend on rice.
Wrong
"Slow eccentrics make up for light weight"Muscle grows from high tension on the fibres that only switch on when a set gets hard. With a light load, what makes a set work is finishing close to failure. Tempo between about 1 and 4 seconds makes little difference; 5–6 second negatives add fatigue, not growth. Keep a controlled 2–3 s lowering and judge each set by how many reps you had left.
Weak
No failure for 4 weeks with an 8 kg dumbbellA set of 8 kg rows stopped far from failure gives your back almost no stimulus. That's a wasted month. Now: 2 easy weeks to manage soreness, then every set ends 1–2 reps short of failure.
Weak
8 kg single-arm row as a main back exerciseThat's maybe 20–25% of what your back can row. You'd need 40+ reps per set. Inverted rows and pull-up negatives use your own body weight, which is the heaviest load you have. The DB row is now the fallback only.
Weak
Bench dips at 113 kgDips put the shoulder into deep extension under most of your body weight, which is a common way to strain the front of the shoulder. They're replaced by overhead triceps extensions. Training the triceps in a stretched, overhead position produced clearly more growth than pushdowns in a 2023 study (Maeo et al.), and 8 kg is a useful load for it.
Weak
Front raises; 9 exercises per upper dayFront delts already get push-ups and presses. Rear delts were the gap. Upper days had 27 sets in a crowded gym, including two curl variations every session. Now 6–7 exercises and about 20 sets, one curl per day.
Missing
Hamstring curls; loading you can increaseNothing bent the knee against resistance, so half of the hamstring's job was untrained. Sliding leg curls fix that for free. Extra load (a backpack, a ₹1,000 band set) is used from week 3, not after week 8, because the 8 kg dumbbell runs out quickly.
Missing
Rules for stalls, breaks, blood work and regainYou've lost 19 kg before and regained it after a layoff. The training program wasn't what failed then. This plan adds weekly decision rules, scheduled maintenance breaks, a minimum routine for bad weeks and a trigger to restart.

Check your start date. "10/08/2026" is 10 August in Indian format. If that's right, you're 9 weeks in. If the scale still reads 113 kg after 9 weeks, the exercise program isn't the problem. Portions are. The usual culprits are rice refills, cooking oil, sugar in coffee and coconut chutney.

03 · Upper / lower, 4 days

Training

Mon · Upper 1Tue · Lower 1Wed · Rest + walkThu · Upper 2Fri · Lower 2Sat · Rest + walkSun · Rest

How hard each set should be

  • Weeks 1–2: 2 sets per exercise, stop with about 3 reps left. You're detrained, and a small first dose prevents most of the soreness that would wreck week 2.
  • Week 3 onward: 3 sets, stop 1–2 reps short of failure. On curls, raises, triceps and push-ups, the last set can go to failure.
  • Never to failure: split squats, single-leg RDLs, pull-up negatives. When balance or control goes, the set is over.
  • Rest 60–90 s on small exercises, 2 min on split squats, rows and push-ups.

Warm-up, 5 minutes

  • Arm circles and band pull-aparts, 20 each
  • Bodyweight squats to a bench, 15
  • Hip hinges with hands on hips, 10
  • One light set of the first exercise

Log every set: exercise, variation, reps, reps left in reserve. Without a log you can't apply the progression rules.

Upper 1

~20 sets · 50 min
  1. 1Push-up (your ladder level)3 × 8–20Use the hardest variation you can do 8+ clean reps of. Body in one line.
  2. 2Pull-up negative3 × 4–6Step up to the top, lower over 4–5 s. You lower about 1.3× what you can lift, so this loads your full body weight now.
  3. 3Inverted row3 × 8–20Bar at chest height to start. Squeeze 1 s at the top. No bar: 8 kg one-arm row with a 2 s hold, 20–30 reps.
  4. 48 kg DB shoulder press3 × 15–30One arm at a time, seated. Take it close to failure; this is where the light load works.
  5. 58 kg lateral raise3 × 12–25Lead with the elbow, stop at shoulder height. Can't get 12 strict? Use one arm, holding a post.
  6. 68 kg DB curl3 × 12–25Elbows pinned, no swing, full stretch at the bottom.
  7. 7Overhead triceps extension3 × 12–258 kg in both hands behind the head. Get the deep stretch at the bottom.

Lower 1 · knee focus

~15 sets · 45 min
  1. 1Bulgarian split squat3 × 8–15/leg3 s down. Bodyweight first; hold the 8 kg once 15 is easy. 113 kg on one leg is already a heavy load.
  2. 2Single-leg RDL, hand on a rail3 × 10–15/leg8 kg in the free hand. Holding a rail removes balance as the limit, so the hamstring does the work.
  3. 3Walking lunge2 × 12–20/legCut from 3 sets; split squats already cover this pattern.
  4. 4Sliding leg curl3 × 6–15Lie on your back, heels on a towel on smooth floor, hips up. Start lowering-only if full reps are too hard.
  5. 5Single-leg calf raise on a step3 × 12–20/leg2 s pause at the bottom stretch.
  6. 6Dead bug2 × 8–12/sideLower back stays pressed into the floor.

Upper 2

~18 sets · 45 min
  1. 1Close-grip push-up3 × 8–20Hands under the shoulders, elbows tucked. Also covers triceps.
  2. 2Underhand inverted row3 × 8–20Palms facing you; more biceps and lower lats.
  3. 3Band-assisted pull-up3 × 6–12No band yet: repeat the negatives from Upper 1.
  4. 4Seated one-arm 8 kg press3 × 15–30Same rules as Upper 1.
  5. 5Rear-delt fly, chest on incline bench3 × 15–258 kg, one arm at a time if needed. Or band face pulls.
  6. 68 kg hammer curl3 × 12–25Last set to failure.

Lower 2 · hip focus

~16 sets · 45 min

  2. 2Deficit reverse lunge3 × 8–15/legStand on a low step, step back and down. Easier on the knees than forward lunges.
  3. 3Single-leg RDL with pause3 × 10–15/leg2 s pause at the bottom, hand on a rail.
  4. 4Heels-up goblet squat, 1.5 reps2 × 12–20Heels on a plate. Down, halfway up, down, all the way up = 1 rep.
  5. 5Sliding leg curl2 × 6–15As in Lower 1.

Weekly hard sets come to about 10–12 for chest, back, quads, hamstrings and glutes, and 6–9 direct for arms and delts. That's enough to keep and slowly build muscle while dieting, and short enough to finish before the gym fills up.

04 · How to make it harder

Progression

Rule: each exercise has a rep range. When every set reaches the top of the range with 1–2 reps still in reserve, move one step right on its ladder next session and drop back to the bottom of the range. Once a set needs more than about 30 reps, burning and breathing end it before the muscle is fully worked. That's when you must move up a step.

Push-up
hands on bench → floor → hands on bricks (deeper) → feet on bench → +5 kg backpack → +10 kg → …
Vertical pull
5 s negatives → band-assisted reps → 1 full rep + negatives → sets of 3–5 → sets of 8+. Every kg you lose makes this easier.
Inverted row
bar at chest height → bar at waist → bar at hip → feet on bench → backpack on chest
Split squat
bodyweight → hold 8 kg → backpack + 8 kg → front foot on a plate (deficit) → add backpack weight
Single-leg RDL / hip thrust
8 kg → backpack → backpack + 8 kg → add pauses
8 kg isolation
12–25 reps → 25–30 reps → 2 s pause at the hardest point → add a band on top of the dumbbell

Buy in this order

  1. Resistance band set (4–5 loop bands, about ₹800–1,500). Pull-up assistance, face pulls, and extra load on curls and presses. The best ₹1,000 you can spend here.
  2. A backpack you already own, filled with books, water bottles or a bag of sand. 10–20 kg for push-ups, split squats and rows. Free.
  3. Digital kitchen scale (about ₹300–500). Needed for the diet, not the gym.
  4. Optional: a cheap adjustable dumbbell set, 20 kg or more.

Be honest about the gym

If any commercial gym within reach costs ₹1,000–1,500 a month, it's the single biggest upgrade available. Heavier loads mean shorter sessions, and keeping real strength numbers is the clearest sign you're holding muscle during the cut. If you can't afford it, the plan above works. It just takes more effort per set.

Deload every 8 weeks: one week at half the sets. Line it up with the diet break.

Soya is cheapest by both money and calories. Chicken and whey are calorie-cheap but cost more. Dal, milk and paneer bring a lot of calories with their protein, so count them as food, not protein sources. Soy at these amounts doesn't lower testosterone in men; the studies on this are consistent. It can cause gas, so build up over a week.

Where Chennai diets leak calories

  • Sugar in coffee/tea: 60–90 kcal a cup. Three cups a day is a meal.
  • Coconut chutney: 2 tbsp ≈ 80–100 kcal. Use sambar, tomato or mint chutney.
  • Ghee roast / oil dosa: +100–150 kcal over a plain dosa.
  • Cooking oil: 1 tbsp = 120 kcal. "1 tsp" means a measuring spoon, not a pour.
  • Meals with unlimited rice: each refill is 200–300 kcal.
  • Parotta 250–300 each, vada 150–200 each, biryani 800–1,200 a plate.
  • Groundnuts: 30 g = 170 kcal. That's why they're out of the snack slot.

Eating out or a festival: pick one plate, protein first, no refills, then get back on plan at the next meal. One 1,000 kcal meal is about 0.1 kg of fat. The damage comes from the "day is ruined anyway" weekend that follows.

06 · Every Sunday, 5 minutes

Weekly rules

Weigh Mon, Wed, Fri: after the toilet, before food, same clothes. Compare weekly averages, never single weigh-ins. One salty meal or a hard leg day can add 1–2 kg of water overnight.

Weeks 1–2Ignore the scale. The first 2–3 kg are water and stored carbs, not fat.
Average loss is 0.5–1% of body weight per week (≈ 0.6–1.1 kg now)Change nothing.
Less than 0.5%/week for 2 weeks and waist isn't shrinkingFirst check honestly: weighed portions, oil, coffee sugar, weekends. If those were on plan, remove 150–200 kcal (about half a cup of rice) or add 2,000 steps. One change at a time.
Scale flat, but waist down and reps upYou're losing fat and gaining muscle. Change nothing for 2 more weeks.
More than 1.2%/week after week 4, or reps falling on 2+ exercises for 2 weeksAdd 150–200 kcal as rice or chapati around training. Losing faster than this costs muscle.
Every 10–12 weeksEat at maintenance for 1–2 weeks (about +600 kcal, mostly carbs). Keep training. Expect +1–2 kg of water on the scale, not fat. This makes the next block easier to stick to.
Sleep under 6.5 h most nightsFix this before cutting calories again. In one controlled study, dieters sleeping 5.5 h lost the same weight as those sleeping 8.5 h, but 55% less of it was fat.

What to track

  • Weight 3× per week → weekly average
  • Waist at the navel, every 2 weeks: relaxed, after a normal breath out, tape level
  • Photos every 2 weeks: same spot, light, pose and time of day
  • Steps daily; sets, reps and reps-in-reserve every session; sleep hours

08 · Things the old plan skipped

Health and recovery

Blood test, once now

South Asians develop diabetes and fatty liver at lower body weights than Europeans. A basic panel in Chennai costs roughly ₹1,000–2,000:

  • HbA1c and fasting glucose
  • Lipid profile
  • Liver enzymes (ALT/SGPT) for fatty liver
  • TSH (thyroid)
  • Vitamin D and B12, both commonly low in India

Retest at week 26. Loud snoring or daytime sleepiness at your weight is worth mentioning to a doctor (sleep apnea).

Supplements worth the money

  • Creatine monohydrate, 3–5 g daily, any time (about ₹5–8/day). The best-supported supplement for keeping strength in a cut. It adds 1–2 kg of water in the first weeks; expect it so you don't panic at the scale.
  • Vitamin D only if the test shows it's low.
  • Whey only on days you can't reach 140 g from food.
  • Skip: fat burners, BCAAs, "cutting" stacks.

Heat: morning sessions in Chennai mean heavy sweating. Drink 3+ litres a day and don't cut salt to zero; cramps and dizziness on lower days usually come from sodium loss.

09 · Because it happened before

Regain plan

Last time, a layoff turned into a regain because there was no smaller routine to fall back on. Plans fail in bad weeks. Decide now what a bad week looks like.

Minimum week (when life gets in the way)

  • 2 home sessions of 25 min: push-ups, backpack split squats, band rows, single-leg hip thrusts
  • 8,000 steps a day
  • 140 g protein; skip the bedtime milk if needed
  • Weigh 3× per week. Never stop weighing.

This won't move you forward much. It keeps you from going backward, and that's the job.

Restart trigger

If your weekly average rises 2 kg above your lowest average, go back to weighing food and the full program the next day. Don't wait until Monday or the end of the month.

After you reach your goal, stay at maintenance for at least 3 months before chasing anything else. Fat lost is easiest to regain in the first year.
